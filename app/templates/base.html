Guide

How It Works

  1. 1 Upload a clear, close-up photo of the skin lesion
  2. 2 Pick your language: English, Urdu, or Arabic
  3. 3 The model classifies it into one of 7 lesion types
  4. 4 Results and explanations appear in your chosen language
  5. 5 Allow location to find nearby dermatologists with phone and website
  6. 6 Download the full PDF report and take it to your doctor

What Runs After Upload

Explainer

Tells you what the condition is in plain language, no medical jargon. Responds in your chosen language.

Risk Triage

Rates urgency as Low, Medium, or Urgent and explains why.

Next Steps

Gives you 4 clear actions to take based on the result.

Dermatologist Finder

Finds up to 5 clinics near you using your location. Click "Get Contact" on any result to see the phone number and website. Click "Maps" to get directions.

PDF Report

Compiles everything into a downloadable report in your chosen language.
